QUANTUM PHANTOMS STUDY
======================


WHAT THIS STUDY IS
------------------
Quantum Phantoms builds synthetic lung tumours without training data: a tumour is placed by anatomy, grown by
published growth models and rendered into a real lung CT with the scanner's own blur and noise, so detection AI
can be tested on rare cases. Every case carries a replayable record of how it was made, and a quantum processor
supplies the seed through a Bell test, which certifies where the randomness came from (provenance, not
realism). The Siemens project ended (2026-11); the continuation drops further quantum claims and measures whether
the synthetic nodules make a detection model better on real scans.


THE 3 METRICS
-------------
1. Detection gain
   Sensitivity of a nodule-detection model on real LIDC scans when trained on real only, real + synthetic and
   synthetic only, split by nodule type, size and position (rare types such as juxtapleural and part-solid are
   the headline).

2. Realism
   Do synthetic nodules match real ones: radiomics feature distributions, intensity (HU) profiles, and whether a
   reader or detector treats them like real nodules.

3. Record integrity
   Replay from the record gives the identical image (voxel hash), and a one-voxel edit is detected. Formal
   properties of the workflow checked as a Petri net (with Korecko).


RESULTS SO FAR AND POSSIBLE RESULTS
-----------------------------------
Measured on ibm_fez (2026-11-14, one calibration snapshot):
- Bell test CHSH 2.68-2.77 on every run (bound 2): the certified seed works (L0 provenance).
- Small quantum separations passed: tick-clock 0.38-0.40 vs 0.296 (5/5 runs), random access codes 0.85 vs 0.75
  and 0.737 vs 0.625, learning with quantum memory passed up to 40 pairs (80 qubits).
- Large entangled growth circuits failed (DSCALE 0-1 of 3, GHZ spine, QCA); classical hardness (L2) not reached.
- Verdict: no stage of the lung pipeline gives quantum a practical job beyond provenance.

Possible:
A. Synthetic rare nodules raise detection sensitivity on real LIDC rare types: a useful result for detection-AI
   makers (SyntheticTumors / Pixel2Cancer line, never done for the lung).
B. Gain only on some types; realism gaps show where the generator is weak.
C. No gain: synthetic data does not transfer; the record and integrity paper still stands (CBMS).


HOW TO VISUALIZE IT
-------------------
1. Before / after CT slices: real host scan, the same scan with the inserted synthetic nodule, and the growth
   sequence (web growth player).
2. Figure: detection sensitivity per nodule type for the three training arms.
3. Figure: radiomics feature distributions, real vs synthetic.
4. The record: one case, its replay hash and the failed check after a one-voxel edit; the Petri net of the
   workflow.


THESIS FIT
----------
Engineering-heavy (A-H pipeline, renderer, record, detector training) with a clear user (detection-AI
validation). Check IP ownership of the code written during the Siemens period before reuse.
Papers: CBMS 2027 (record + Petri net), then MICCAI / ISBI / SPIE MI (synthetic lung nodules + detection gain).
