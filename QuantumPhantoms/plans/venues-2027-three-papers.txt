1. IEEE CBMS 2027 (ROME): THE RECORD PAPER
------------------------------------------

1.1 Facts
- 40th IEEE International Symposium on Computer-Based Medical Systems.
- 23-25 June 2027, Universita Campus Bio-Medico di Roma. In person only; one author must present.
- Paper deadline 20 January 2027. Notification 5 March 2027. Camera-ready 15 March 2027.
- Regular paper: up to 6 pages, two-column IEEE template. (Short: 4 pages. Poster: 2 pages.)
- Matching topics from the call: "Privacy, security, research ethics and regulatory aspects of
  medical technologies"; "Digital twins and personalized computational models"; "Explainable,
  trustworthy and responsible AI in medicine"; "Real-world clinical evaluation, implementation and
  validation of computer-based systems".
- Open points to ask the program chairs before submitting:
    a) The website says "IEEE sponsorship pending approval". Will the proceedings be in IEEE Xplore?
    b) No preprint policy is stated. Is an arXiv preprint allowed?
- An ethics statement is required. Ours: public data only (LIDC-IDRI), no new patient data.

1.2 Working title
"A replayable and formally checked record for synthetic lung nodules seeded by a quantum processor"

1.3 The one question
Can every synthetic CT case carry a record from which a third party can (a) regenerate the same
voxels, (b) see any later edit, and (c) trace the random seed to one identified hardware job?

1.4 Content, section by section (6 pages)
  1. Introduction (0.5 page). Synthetic cases are used to test detection AI. A test case is only
     evidence if its origin can be checked. State the three contributions.
  2. Requirements (0.5 page). A short list of what a manufacturer needs from such a record.
     Source: Michal Baca. Written in the words "traceability", "audit trail", "regulatory evidence".
  3. Pipeline (1 page). Stages A to H in one figure. One paragraph per stage. State clearly which
     stages are classical (A, E, F, G, H) and which use the quantum processor (B, C, D).
  4. Formal model (1.5 pages). The workflow as a Petri net. Properties checked:
       P1  No tile is rendered unless the Bell test (CHSH) passed for its seed.
       P2  A failed Bell test (S <= 2) ends the draw: no seed, no retry.
       P3  Every record refers to exactly one hardware job.
       P4  No randomness-beacon pulse is used twice.
       P5  Replay from the record always reaches the same final state.
     Source: Stefan Korecko (formalism, tool, proof method).
  5. Experiment (1.5 pages). One tumour through A to H on real hardware.
       - Replay test: regenerate the case from the record; voxel hash must be identical.
       - Integrity test: change one voxel; the check must fail.
       - Seed comparison: quantum seed against pseudo-random seed. Expected result: no difference
         in the image. Say this plainly. The quantum part gives provenance, not image quality.
       - Cost: QPU seconds, wall time, record size.
  6. Limits and conclusion (1 page with references). One host scan, one case, no reader study.

1.5 What must exist before 20 January
  [ ] The refactored pipeline runs dry, end to end (nothing has been executed since 2026-11-11).
  [ ] The A-to-H chain is closed (job append in G, D founder from B's draw, beacon rule in C).
  [ ] One live run with the job ID inside the record, plus 3 to 5 repeats.
  [ ] The Petri net model and the five property results.
  [ ] The replay test and the one-voxel test, run on the live case.
  [ ] Half a page of requirements from Michal Baca.
  [ ] Answers from the CBMS chairs to the two open points.
  [ ] Siemens publication clearance, if any Siemens name or affiliation appears. Ask in November.

1.6 People
- Peter: pipeline, hardware runs, experiment, writing.
- Stefan Korecko: formal model, study design review, senior co-author.
- Michal Baca: requirements section. Co-author if he writes it, acknowledgment if he only advises.
  This needs his agreement and his employer's clearance.

1.7 Wording limits
- Never write that quantum computing makes the images better or more realistic.
- The certificate is a statement about the device, not about the tumour.
- Privacy comes from building cases out of parameters only. It does not come from the certificate.
- Siemens data is not used. The host scan is a public LIDC case from a Siemens scanner.


2. IEEE ISBI 2027 (LAUSANNE): THE IMAGE ABSTRACT
------------------------------------------------

2.1 Facts
- 25-28 May 2027, SwissTech Convention Center, EPFL. Fully in person.
- One-page abstract. Deadline 1 February 2027. Notification 15 February 2027.
- Poster only. Not in the proceedings, not indexed.
- Submission through EDAS. An ethics statement is required.
- Matching topics: "Generative models and synthetic data in biomedical imaging"; "Physical,
  biological, and statistical modelling"; "Image quality assessment and harmonization".
- No workshop or special session at ISBI 2027 covers quantum computing or synthetic CT.

2.2 Working title
"Training-free lung nodule insertion that keeps the host scanner's blur and noise"

2.3 The one question
Does a nodule that was grown by a published growth model and inserted into a real CT scan match
the host scan in resolution and noise, and does a detector respond to it as to a real nodule?

2.4 Content (one page)
- Method, 4 to 5 sentences: placement in the lung, cellular-automaton growth, partial-volume
  rendering, insertion with the host's measured blur and noise. No training data.
- One figure: host slice before, after, and a real nodule of similar size next to it.
- Two or three numbers:
    volume error of the inserted nodule against the target;
    noise level inside and around the nodule against the host background;
    response of an open nodule detector to synthetic and to real nodules (if ready).
- One sentence on the quantum part: the random seed comes from a certified hardware job and is
  stored in a replayable record (reference to the preprint).
- This abstract must not repeat the CBMS paper. It contains no formal model and no record tests.

2.5 What must exist before 1 February
  [ ] Rendered cases in the Siemens-scanner LIDC host, and ideally two more hosts.
  [ ] The numbers above, measured on the current pipeline, not copied from older runs.
  [ ] The detector comparison, or an honest statement that it is planned.

2.6 Why go at all
The abstract has almost no value on a CV. The trip has value only through meetings:
  [ ] Three or four named imaging researchers to meet at the poster (synthetic data, virtual
      imaging trials, lung CT). Write to them in April.
  [ ] One imaging or medical co-author for the next paper. This is the largest gap of the project.
  [ ] A seminar or a meeting at IBM Research Zurich and at ETH in the same week. Ask by February,
      through Stefan Korecko or the Siemens contact, and bring the preprint.
If the abstract is rejected, register and go anyway. The meetings do not depend on it.

3. IEEE QCE 2027 (RALEIGH): THE HARDWARE PAPER
----------------------------------------------

3.1 Facts
- IEEE Quantum Week, 10-15 October 2027, Raleigh, NC, USA.
- Deadline not published yet. It was 31 March in 2025 and 20 April in 2026. Expect April 2027 (verify).
- 2025 format: 8 to 10 pages plus 2 pages of references. IEEE Xplore proceedings. (verify for 2027)
- Call page: https://qce.quantum.ieee.org/callfortechnicalpapers/

3.2 Working title
"A quantum-seeded virtual tumour pipeline: what one Heron processor certifies today"
(This is Paper 2 of plans/publication-plan-3-papers.md.)

3.3 The one question
Inside a working imaging pipeline, which quantum claims hold on current hardware when measured
with repeats, and which fail?

3.4 Content, section by section
  1. Introduction. A hybrid job in which the quantum processor is used only where it has a
     measured task.
  2. Claim levels, defined once and mapped to standard names: certified randomness, entanglement
     witness, memory advantage, sampling hardness. Each with its classical bound.
  3. Circuits: the cohort roll with the Bell test, the entangled seed-shape circuit, the others.
  4. Hardware results with repeats, each number with backend, job ID and classical bound:
       - Bell test on the roll. Single job so far: S = 2.733 on ibm_marrakesh.
       - Cellular-automaton witness: marginal so far.
       - GHZ spine: failed.
       - Process-memory circuit: lost to the classical model.
  5. Design rules learned from the failures: terminal readout only, calibration-based qubit
     pruning, spend guard, rejection of dead qubits.
  6. Cost ledger: QPU seconds per case, and why the present circuits are classically simulable.
  7. The pipeline and the record in brief, citing the CBMS paper.
  8. Limits. No claim of advantage. No claim about image quality.

3.5 What must exist before April
  [ ] 3 to 5 repeats of every hardware number, on at least one backend, better two.
  [ ] The same statistical test for every claim, fixed before the runs.
  [ ] A classical baseline cohort for comparison.
  [ ] All figures produced by scripts from the stored runs.
  [ ] The CBMS paper accepted or at least on arXiv, so that it can be cited.

3.6 People
- Peter: all hardware work and writing.
- Stefan Korecko: senior co-author, study design.
- Useful but not required: one reader with a quantum information background to check section 2.

3.7 Minimum that still publishes
Certified randomness holds with repeats; the entangled-seed claims are marginal or fail; the
reasons are given. A negative result with repeats is acceptable at this venue. Single jobs are not.
