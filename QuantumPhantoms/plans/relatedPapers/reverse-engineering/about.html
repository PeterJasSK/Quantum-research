Quantum Phantoms: reverse engineering

What this folder is for: rebuild 21 published methods from their papers, one pipeline stage at a time, so that every stage of the synthetic-lung-tumor pipeline rests on a reproduced, cited method instead of a guess.

Quantum Phantoms generates synthetic lung tumors inside real 3-D chest CT. Their randomness comes from a quantum job whose statistics are certified, and every step after that job is signed and replayable. This folder is independent of the MVP (QuantumPhantoms/code/ is never imported). Each technique is re-derived from its paper, checked by reproducing one published number, and then adapted to lungs, certification and signing. The results are building blocks that later become the real pipeline.

The pipeline, A to H

Purple stages (B, C, D) contain quantum circuits; the rest are classical. Each box lists the contract files the stage writes. Letters exchange data only through these files, which are frozen in shared/contracts/. Every contract has a valid synthetic example in shared/stubs/, so each letter can be built before its upstream exists. The seed chain runs: certified counts (C) → extracted seed → shared.seedhash.rng → every PRNG in E and F. Everything after the quantum job is deterministic and replayable from G's receipt.

Why reverse-engineer

For every stage, some published method already solves part of the problem: a Bayesian-network compiler, Bell-test entropy accounting, quantum causal-state models, Pixel2Cancer, scanner fingerprinting, VICTRE and others. Rebuilding each method ourselves, from the paper and without copying proprietary code, gives us components we understand, can cite, and can adapt to lungs. Every target goes through the same lifecycle:

  1. Dissect: a dossier with method, equations, parameters and claimed result.
  2. Reproduce: match one published number or figure in repro/ before changing anything.
  3. Adapt: to lungs, 3-D, certification and our contracts.
  4. Integrate, or reject with a written reason.

States move listed → dissected → reproduced → adapted → integrated | rejected, recorded per letter in <L>_<name>/status.json and shown as badges in the index.

Claim ladder: what "quantum" is allowed to mean here

LevelClaimWhereStatus
L0Provenance: the randomness came from a CHSH-checked quantum source and is accounted for (certified bits → extracted seed).C, B-1Reachable
L1Entangled generative state, shown by a multi-qubit witness (e.g. ⟨XX⟩+⟨ZZ⟩) read in ≥ 2 bases.B-2, D-2, D-3Reachable
L1′Quantum memory advantage: the causal-state model of a growth process needs less memory than any classical one (C_q < C_μ). Headline result.D-5Reachable
L2Classical hardness.noneBlocked: shallow heavy-hex circuits are belief-propagation-simulable (Tindall). Target #9 turns this into a measured number.
L3Fault-tolerant algorithms (Carleman PDE, amplitude estimation).noneNeeds fault-tolerant hardware; out of scope.

Never claim "more realistic because quantum", "quantum biology", or "classically impossible". A single-qubit phase dial measured in Z is a classical biased coin, so any interference claim must sit in multi-qubit correlations read in at least two bases. Every quantum deliverable also ships its classical baseline and its belief-propagation simulability number.

Simulation only

No IBM jobs run in this epic. Every circuit is designed as if it had to run on Heron r2: it is transpiled to the heavy-hex coupling map and CZ basis of FakeMarrakesh, all-to-all registers stay ≤ 15–20 qubits, and mid-circuit readouts are kept to a minimum because readout error is much larger than two-qubit error. It is then executed on AerSimulator in two modes: ideal, and with the FakeMarrakesh noise model. Every quantum number carries the label simulated: ideal or simulated: Heron-class noise (FakeMarrakesh), and the contract schemas reject any record without it.

Simulated CHSH certifies nothing. C delivers the accounting pipeline validated on simulated data; every certificate says certification-ready, not certified (simulated). Real certification needs a hardware run, which is future work. If S ≤ 2, the draw is rejected and logged, with no retry and no fallback.

Privacy by construction

No patient voxels enter any generator. Host CT scans (LIDC-IDRI, later Mayo Siemens raw data) are only read: to choose a site, measure the scanner fingerprint, and insert into. The tumor's appearance comes from fitted parameters (shape, edge profile, HU per nodule type) and published statistics only. Learned refiners trained on patients (DiffTumor, MAISI) appear only as ablations.

How the work is organised

  • W (done first, alone) froze the contracts, stubs, shared helpers, letter skeletons, this explainer and the site build. shared/ is now read-only until INT.
  • Wave 1: A, C, E, F; Wave 2: B, D, G, H. Each letter runs in its own git worktree and Claude session (git worktree add ../qp-re-<L> -b re-<L>), writes only inside its own folder, and records any contract change it needs as a request in its README.
  • INT replaces the stubs with real upstream files (A → B → C/D → E → F → G), fixes contract mismatches in one place, rebuilds the site, and runs H on one tumor that travels A → H with a verifying receipt.
  • The Markdown READMEs and dossiers are the source of truth. python -m shared.site.build renders them, with each letter's status.json, into index.html and site/.
