What you are watching

Each organism's soma is a quantum walker on the 5-site track (amber = A, cyan = B). They start apart and hop toward each other. A coherent XX+YY exchange lives permanently at the shared sites; it moves excitation between bodies only where both have amplitude — emergent contact, never a coded branch.

The germ lines — the GHZ genotypes that carry the entanglement witness — are off-track and untouched. The witness readout is the whole experiment: does contact between bodies cost the certified genealogy?

What the three arms show

No contact — walkers pass through each other. Entropy stays 0.00: the coupling does nothing until bodies meet. Witness flat at +1.00. The control.

Soma–soma — bodies collide, entropy jumps to ~2 bits (they really interacted), yet the witness stays +1.00. The barrier holds through collision.

Germ-routed A/B — route the same contact through a germ qubit and the witness collapses to ≈−0.17. Same kill-switch PJ0 found, now cross-organism.
