QUANTUM ARTIFICIAL LIFE (artificial-life/) STUDY
================================================


WHAT THIS STUDY IS
------------------
We rebuild the quantum artificial life model of Alvarez-Rodriguez et al. (2018) - organisms with a genotype and
phenotype that self-replicate, mutate, interact and die - as one circuit on IBM Heron, at far larger scale than
the original 4 qubits. The only part that is genuinely quantum is the entanglement passed down a lineage; every
count of who is alive, how long they live or how deep a lineage goes has an exact classical copy. The study asks
how much Darwinian biology (reproduction, selection, metabolism, death, movement) can be added before that
quantum signature collapses to classical.


THE 3 METRICS
-------------
1. Entanglement depth W*
   The largest lineage (number of generations W, 2 qubits each) whose genealogy witness <X^W> stays above the
   classical separable bound by k sigma (k = 2, also reported at k = 3).

2. Two-basis certificate
   <XX> + <ZZ> on the germ line (separable bound 1, ideal 2). Unlike the single-basis witness it excludes
   classically correlated mixtures, so it is the honest "this organism is quantum" test.

3. Richness vs survival
   For each biological process added (death, selection, metabolism, movement, collision), the depth at which the
   witness is lost: the phase diagram of richness against quantum survival.


RESULTS SO FAR AND POSSIBLE RESULTS
-----------------------------------
Measured:
- Months 1-3 metric C(g) refuted: it is a diagonal quantity with an exact classical surrogate; teleport routing
  lost to SWAP on Heron r2 (readout error now dominates two-qubit error), refuted twice.
- Scale: W* = 24 generations = 48 qubits on ibm_kingston, about 6x the 2018 paper; W = 32 dead.
- Moving organism (PJ2.4): reproduction, selection, metabolism and unitary death all act while it moves; the
  two-basis GHZ certificate survived on ibm_marrakesh (<XX> + <ZZ> = 1.96 vs bound 1).
- Open caveat: the W50 witness is single-basis, not genuine multipartite entanglement (fix planned).

Possible:
A. Richness map: each process has a measured cost in depth; a certified organism with the full biology at W > 24.
B. Error mitigation (dynamical decoupling on the germ line, PJ2.5) pushes W* out.
C. Null: every extra process costs so much that only the bare model stays certified; still a clean boundary.
No speed advantage is claimed anywhere; the claim is faithfulness and certified scale.

THESIS FIT
----------
Strong engineering (full model as one circuit, hardware driver, certification statistics, web vivarium) and an
honest research question with measured hardware results. Basis of the own paper "evolution frontier"
(THESIS/QuantumEvolutionFrontier/).
