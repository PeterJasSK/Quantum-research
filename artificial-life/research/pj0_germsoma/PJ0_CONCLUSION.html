The germ/soma split moves the ceiling from biology to qubit count

Heron-r2 · ibm_kingston (156q) witness <X⊗W> (genotype-only) ▲ Stage PJ0 — germ/soma, natural + separable 2026-09-12

Abstract

P2 drove the faithful 2018 death channel to its absolute limit: with real amplitude-damping death in the loop, the genealogical entanglement witness collapsed at W* = 9 = 19 qubits. The diagnosis was mechanical — the death operator entangles the mortal soma to the immortal germ line (cx(g,p)) then dissipates it, so body-death irreversibly decoheres the gene-witness. That was a biology wall, not a hardware one.

PJ0 builds the Weismann barrier: the germ line carries the GHZ witness and is kept coherent; the soma expresses the trait as a separable diagonal state and dies in isolation (natural T1 decay, no bath, no cx(g,p)). The soma-to-germ coupling is severed, so soma death can no longer touch the witness. A 2×2 factorial confirmed this live: at W=4, separable keeps the witness alive (0.90) while re-entangling the soma collapses it (0.12).

Sweeping the severed design in width, the witness stays certified at k=3σ through W=50 = 100 qubits — and the run did not stop because the entanglement died. It stopped at W=54 because the chip ran out of clean qubits: the layout needs a SWAP-free chain of 108, and ibm_kingston's longest clean chain is 107. The limiting resource flipped from entanglement to machine size. That is the PJ0 result.

Honest framing: the witness is certified, not strong, at the top of the sweep (+0.058 at W=50, ~5σ over the classical null but far below the ~0.9 of a fresh GHZ). This is scale · faithfulness · certification — not a speedup, and each width is a single N=1 snapshot.

1 · Fewer 2-qubit gates = less error = higher witness at the same width. natural+sep carries one 2-qubit gate per individual (the clone); damping adds cry+cx, entangled adds cx(g,p). Two-qubit gates dominate hardware error, so the leanest circuit reads the strongest witness — exactly what 0.899 > 0.768 shows.

2 · Qubit cost is the scalability lever — this is WHY W=50 was reachable. natural has no bath: 2 qubits/individual vs 3. On the 107-qubit clean chain that is ~53 individuals vs ~35. The W=54 qubit wall (§3) is a natural-mode wall at 2W; damping would hit its own wall ~1.5× sooner. Leanness bought the scale result.

3 · Faithfulness — the stated goal. natural death = real hardware T1 relaxation toward |0⟩; no injected bath, no cry simulating decay. The trait is classical by the 2018 paper's own text, so a diagonal soma dying by real physics is the honest model — the engineered bath is a simulation of one.

4 · The only thing damping wins: reproducibility. natural's rate = hardware T1, which drifts run-to-run and is not tunable; damping's γ is a controllable knob. That is the sole reason local_damping is kept — as a rigid-rate reference/fallback, not the headline.

3 · The wall

W=54 aborts on qubit count, verbatim

The abort is explicit and physical — a chain-length failure, not a decoherence gate trip:

Run log — W=54, steps=27

Backend : ibm_kingston  (156 qubits)
=== PJ0 germ/soma: widths=[54] steps=27 soma_death=natural phenotype=separable ===
[PJ0 ABORT] no clean chain of 108 qubits: no clean SWAP-free chain of 108
qubits on ibm_kingston (longest found 107; edges >= 0.1 2q-err pruned);
dead=[112, 113, 120, 121, 146]. Lower genome N_SLOTS so N_BITS <= 107, or
pin a better-calibrated --backend.

Each individual costs 2 qubits (germ + soma, no bath in natural mode), so W=54 needs 108. After pruning edges above the 0.1 two-qubit-error bound and dropping five dead qubits (112, 113, 120, 121, 146), the longest clean SWAP-free chain on this calibration was 107 — one short. The gate holds; the chip does not. A better-calibrated backend, a longer clean chain, or error mitigation would push straight past W=54 — and the open question becomes whether the true entanglement ceiling of this design is even reachable on a 156-qubit device.

4 · Confound & honesty

Certified is not the same as strong — what this run does and does not prove

What it proves. The classical separable null ∏i<Xi> stayed ≤ 10⁻¹³ across the sweep — orders of magnitude under the CD-3 gate — so every reported witness is genuinely non-classical genealogical entanglement, and it is present at k=3σ out to 100 qubits. The soma-severing mechanism works exactly as the A/B predicted: no cx(g,p), no witness-collapsing death channel.

What it does not prove. The witness is certified but weak at the top: +0.058 at W=50 is ~5σ over the null but far below a fresh GHZ's ~0.9. The decay is real and steep, and the top decade sits close to the gate. Every width is a single N=1 snapshot on one calibration — the non-monotonic points (W=16 at 0.296, W=28 at 0.355) are run-to-run chain-quality noise, not physics. The sweep also scales depth with width (steps ≈ W/2), so it does not isolate a pure width ceiling. And the runs carried calibration:null / t1_band:null — the aging-order-deviation and selective-DD placement checks were not populated, so PJ0's irregular-lifespan claim remains unverified here.

The honest headline is therefore precise: at the width where the hardware forced the run to stop, the entanglement was still certified. The machine ran out before the witness did. Whether the witness would survive beyond W=54 with more clean qubits is the next experiment, not a settled claim.

5 · Decision gate

PJ0 healed the P2 wound — the bottleneck is now hardware, and PJ1 can build the arena

Gate ruling

The P2 biology wall is gone. Faithful damping death capped the witness at W*=9 because soma death decohered the germ line. The germ/soma split severs that coupling; the certified ceiling moved past W=50 = 100 qubits and the run terminated on a qubit-count limit (needs 108 clean, chip has 107), not on entanglement collapse.

Design decision confirmed by data. natural + separable is faithful (real T1 death, classical soma per the 2018 paper, genotype-only witness), leaner (2W qubits → scales), and defensible (the 2×2 A/B shows severing is the mechanism, not a dodge).

Open before write-up. (1) Repeats + a reported T1 band to turn N=1 snapshots into error bars; (2) a better-calibrated / longer-chain backend or error mitigation to find the true width ceiling past W=54; (3) backend-scheduled runs to populate the aging-order and selective-DD checks. Then PJ1 tiles these 2W-qubit organisms into the multi-lineage arena and reads the joint cross-lineage witness.
