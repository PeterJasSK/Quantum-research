Abstract

Phase 1 banked the exact Alvarez-Rodriguez 2018 four-operator model and its one non-classical observable, the genealogical entanglement witness <X⊗W>. Phase 2 pushes that witness to the coherence ceiling of live 156-qubit Heron-r2 hardware along two axes: the unitary genealogy (idealized, no dissipation) and the faithful amplitude-damping death channel — the first genuine biological operator switched on.

On the unitary axis the reproduction certifies entanglement depth W* = 24 = 48 qubits (k=2σ), about 6× the paper's ~4-qubit origin; W=32 is buried in noise. Turning on the amplitude-damping death channel is far harsher: after a damping-model fix (0.35 → 0.55 at W=2) the witness is now certified at k=3σ through W=4 (W=2 +0.545, W=3 +0.782, W=4 +0.648) but has collapsed to +0.001 by W=8 — placing the death-channel ceiling W* in W ∈ [4, 8). The collapse edge (W=5, W=6) remains to be run (§6).

Honest framing throughout: this is scale · faithfulness · certification — not a speedup.

1 · The two axes

Scale is qubit-cheap; the wall is decoherence — and the death operator makes the wall much closer

Each individual costs two qubits; the amplitude-damping death mode adds one shared bath ancilla. Width is therefore never the bottleneck — a genealogy of W=77 would fill a 156-qubit chip. What ends the witness is entropy: decoherence along the unitary axis, and, once the faithful death channel is on, the deliberate dissipation that channel injects at every generation.

P2 measures the witness against genealogy width W on both axes. The unitary axis (§2) finds how far the idealized reproduction coheres. The damping axis (§3) is the first honest biological operator — it re-asks the ceiling question with real death in the loop, and the answer moves dramatically inward.

2 · The unitary scale ceiling

W* = 24 = 48 qubits, ~6× the paper

On the unitary genealogy the witness decays roughly geometrically with W. Against the noiseless ideal curve — which saturates near 0.9 (its small droop is modeled mutation, Ry(θ), not decoherence) — the hardware curve separates decoherence from model-intrinsic decay. The last width clearing the k=2σ significance gate is W=24; W=32 is dead.

3 · The amplitude-damping death channel

Real death moves the ceiling inward — and a model fix recovered it from 0.35 to 0.55

Switching death from a unitary rotation to a faithful amplitude-damping channel (a shared bath ancilla, dissipation per generation) is the first genuine biological operator of the epic — and the harshest noise channel in the model. The original damping build was unstable at W=2 (runs spanning −0.04 to +0.35). A death-channel fix lifted the W=2 witness to a solid +0.545 (k=3σ certified) — the "0.35 → 0.55" improvement. The fixed channel then holds: W=3 = +0.782 and W=4 = +0.648 are both certified at k=3σ (the W=3 rise above W=2 reflects the earlier W=2 runs sitting on a noisier chain, not a real minimum). The collapse falls between W=4 and W=8: by W=8 the witness is +0.001, dead. Live calibration on the W=3/W=4 runs: two-qubit error ≈ 0.002, readout ≤ 0.012 — the decay is the death channel, not a bad chain.

4 · Confound & significance

The decay is physical, not a dead-edge artifact

Every hardware run enforced the fail-closed chain-quality gate (abort if two-qubit error > 0.05 or readout > 0.15), so the witness decay is device physics, not a broken edge. The separable null ∏i<Xi> — what a classical measure-and-resend device returns — stayed ≤ 2×10⁻⁶ across all widths on both axes, five orders of magnitude under the CD-3 gate of 0.05. The witness is the sole quantum claim; every diagonal metric (alive-count, deepest-lineage, phenotype ⟨σ_z⟩) has an exact classical surrogate and carries none.

5 · Decision gate (AC-P2.4)

Unitary scale exhausted; the death channel opens the real richness question

Gate ruling — evaluated gradually

Unitary scale axis: exhausted. W* = 24 proven, W=32 dead, decay geometric with no structure. Further unitary width is "more qubits, same biology" — no new scientific content.

Death-channel axis: not exhausted — this is the live frontier. The faithful amplitude-damping genealogy now certifies through W=4 (+0.65) and collapses by W=8, so its ceiling is bracketed to W ∈ [4, 8) but not located. Per CD-11, error mitigation is the first lever to push that W* out before any further biology. The gate toward the P3 richness menu opens through the death channel: pin the collapse edge (W=5, W=6) and spend mitigation on it first; broader biology stays deferred.
