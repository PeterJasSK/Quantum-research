the metaphor

Growing a lineage, one generation at a time

Read left to right as generations 1 → 24, numbered on the line. The founder (gen 1) lights alone, fully coherent — the ancestral genotype. It replicates, copying its genotype into the next generation; that child mutates by a quantum-random angle and interacts with its neighbour, and the line steps on — gen 2, 3, 4 … out to gen 24, depth W=24, 48 qubits, the headline. Every generation stays lit once born, so the whole lineage accumulates, then resets and grows again.

The violet inheritance thread is the shared entanglement passed down the line. Its brightness is the measured witness ⟨X⊗W⟩: the founder's bond is strongest, and each further generation inherits a weaker share — bright early, half-lit near gen 12, a faint ember by gen 24 (witness 0.038). So the deeper the lineage, the fainter its quantum coherence — the exact fade the hardware measured. Green individuals are alive; a red ✕ marks one whose phenotype aged to the dark |0⟩ state and died (the count matches the measured alive-count).

How it works

How it lives the Darwinian way

An individual is 2 qubits: a genotype g and a phenotype p. The whole 2018 model is four Darwinian operators, each a specific gate, verified operator-by-operator against the paper's closed-form values.

OperatorGateMeaning

Why one number is quantum and the others are not. The population's alive-count is a ⟨σz⟩ quantity living on the density-matrix diagonal — a classical genotype-copy plus value-tracking reproduces it exactly. The quantum claim lives only off-diagonal, in the joint X-parity witness: a GHZ genealogy gives ⟨X⊗W⟩ = 1 while any separable line gives ∏⟨X⟩ ≈ 0. That gap has no classical surrogate — which is why it, and only it, is the headline.

What we did not get

The honest ledger

The population metrics are classical no quantum claim

Alive-count and deepest-lineage are diagonal observables. A death-matched classical surrogate reproduces them to Δ ≈ 0. They give the visual its life, but they carry no quantum signal — every quantum claim on this page is the witness, nothing else.

No quantum-speed advantage scale, not speedup

This is a faithfulness-and-scale milestone: the full four-operator model, rebuilt and pushed to 48 qubits of provably non-classical genealogical entanglement. It is not a claim that the quantum machine computes anything faster than a classical one.
