Reading the hero — what you are watching

Body A comes in from the left, body B from the right. They walk together and collide — every frame is a real measurement on the QPU — while each keeps its certified quantum genealogy alive.

The picture is a position histogram of the two bodies as they move along a track, rebuilt from 8,192 shots per frame. Here is every mark on it:

The 8 bands = the 8-site track. Each vertical band is one position the bodies can occupy on the shared 8-site lattice. Left band = site 0, right band = site 7.
Amber bars = body A's occupancy. Each bar's height is the measured probability of finding organism A at that site this frame. A starts pinned at the far left.
Cyan bars = body B's occupancy. Same reading for the second organism, which starts pinned at the far right. Two colours = two separate germ/soma individuals.
The glowing continuous lines = the same bars, smoothed. Each body's per-site probabilities drawn as one envelope, so the organism reads as a single moving wave-packet rather than eight separate bars. One line per body, in its own colour.
The white flares = contact. Where A and B both have amplitude at the same site, they overlap. The glow is white because it belongs to neither colour — it is the two of them meeting. This is the collision, measured: overlap climbs 0.00 → 0.72 across the run.
The labelled A / B heads = each body's centre. The ringed dot with a dropline marks where each organism currently peaks on the track — its position — so you can watch the two centres walk toward each other and meet.

Meanwhile the top-right cards and the bottom heartbeat show the one thing a classical computer cannot fake: the genealogy witness ⟨X⊗2W⟩ over both germ lines, staying alive above the null (≈0) through the whole collision.

The win · P2 + PJ0 · the scale ceiling

An old design hit a wall.
A new design moved it.

P2 drove the faithful 2018 death channel to its limit: with real amplitude-damping death in the loop, the genealogical witness collapsed at W* = 9 = 19 qubits. Mechanical diagnosis — the death operator couples the mortal soma to the immortal germ line (cx(g,p)) then dissipates it, so body-death irreversibly decoheres the gene-witness. A biology wall, not a hardware one.

PJ0 builds the Weismann barrier: the germ line carries the GHZ witness and stays coherent; the soma is a separable diagonal trait dying in isolation (natural T1, no bath, no cx(g,p)). Severing that one coupling moved the certified ceiling to W = 50 = 100 qubits (k=3σ) — the run stopped only because the chip ran out of clean qubits (needs a 108-chain; longest clean is 107). The machine ran out before the witness did — the limiting resource flipped from entanglement to machine size.

Honest: the witness is certified, not strong, at the top (+0.058 at W=50, ~5σ over null, far below a fresh GHZ's ~0.9). Scale · faithfulness · certification — not a speedup. N=1 per width. The A/B one-liner: at W=4 the severed design reads +0.899; restoring the old cx(g,p) wound collapses it to +0.120. The coupling is the mechanism.

What is happening · PJ1 · the arena

They collided on real hardware — and the genealogy survived.

The run: IBM ibm_kingston (156-qubit Heron r2), 42 qubits = 2×(W12 germ + 8 body + 1 trait), 12 measured frames, 8,192 shots/frame, QRNG-certified (fail-closed), chain-quality gate passed (twoq_err_max 0.0073, readout_max 0.026), selective DD on both germ lines, ~1 min QPU.

Why they are organisms: each is a germ/soma individual — a clean 12-qubit quantum genealogy (the germ line, carrying certified entanglement inheritance) plus a moving body (the soma) on the track. Two of them.

How they collided: the bodies start at opposite ends of an 8-site track and walk toward each other; the measured occupancy overlap climbs monotonically 0.00 → 0.72 (Z-basis, off the chip) — a real collision, not an if (contact) branch; one physical coupling is always on and only acts where both bodies have amplitude.

What held: the joint witness ⟨X⊗2W⟩ over both 24 germ qubits stayed alive across all 12 frames, +0.16…+0.50, mean +0.35, far above the separable null (≈0; cross-lineage cut −0.003). The Weismann barrier on a QPU: bodies collide, gene lines do not.

The inherited method

the claim

One certified witness

The sole quantum claim is an entanglement witness (⟨X⊗W⟩) with no classical surrogate, checked against a separable null and a significance gate (k=2σ headline / k=3σ reported). Alive-counts, ⟨σz⟩, occupancy — all have exact classical surrogates, so carry no quantum claim.

the discipline

Sim-first, hardware-confirm

Fix in statevector sim, then confirm live. Every hardware run is gated: certified QRNG entropy (fail-closed), and a chain-quality gate that aborts if two-qubit error > 0.05 or readout > 0.15. Selective dynamical decoupling protects the gene line only.

the honesty

No speedup claimed

The contribution is scale · faithfulness · certification — not a quantum advantage. Negatives are reported as readings: the σ frontier, the qubit wall, a witness certified, not strong at the top of a sweep. Body-body entanglement is labelled sim-only. Each width is a single N=1 snapshot.
