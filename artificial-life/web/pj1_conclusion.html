Two organisms collided on real quantum hardware

The PJ1 arena run certified on IBM ibm_kingston (156-qubit Heron). Two germ/soma organisms — each a clean 12-qubit quantum genealogy plus a moving body — placed on a shared 8-site track, their bodies started at opposite ends and walked toward each other over 12 measured frames. Every frame is a real quantum circuit measured at 8,192 shots. This is not a simulation.

The run. backend ibm_kingston · 42 qubits (2×(W12 germ + 8 body + 1 trait)) · 12 frames · 8,192 shots/frame · QRNG-certified (fail-closed) · chain-quality gate passed (twoq_err_max 0.0073, readout_max 0.026) · selective dynamical decoupling on both germ lines. 12 jobs, ~1 min QPU.

They overlapped — measured on the QPU

Body occupancy is read in the Z basis (robust to noise) every frame. The two bodies' centroids converge and their occupancy overlap climbs monotonically 0.00 → 0.72: they demonstrably reach the same region of the track and collide. This is a direct hardware measurement, not a model.

The genealogy survived the collision

The joint genealogy witness ⟨X⊗2W⟩ (24 germ qubits, X basis) stayed alive through all 12 frames — range +0.16 to +0.50, mean +0.35, always far above the separable joint null (≈0). The cross-lineage cut witness sat at ≈0 (−0.003) throughout. On real hardware, through a full body collision, each organism kept its certified quantum genealogy. That is the Weismann barrier holding on a QPU: the bodies collide, the gene lines do not.

What this proves on hardware. Two organisms collided on a real quantum computer — occupancy overlap 0.00 → 0.72, measured — and each retained a live entanglement-certified genealogy (witness +0.16…+0.50 ≫ null) across the whole encounter. Certified quantum entropy (QRNG), fail-closed, chain-quality gated.

Honest scope

What this does NOT claim. (1) The witness does not respond to the contact — by construction the soma_soma coupling never touches a germ qubit, so the genealogy is insulated from the collision; its frame-to-frame wiggle is per-frame transpilation/readout noise, not the bodies acting on the genes (that insulation is the point). (2) The body–body entanglement on contact is not measured here: it needs a statevector the 42-qubit register is too large for, so the entanglement reading is sim-only. Hardware proves the meeting (occupancy overlap) and the genealogy survival (witness); it does not prove the bodies entangled.
