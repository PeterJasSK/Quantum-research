Abstract

Phase 1 banked the exact Alvarez-Rodriguez 2018 four-operator model and its one non-classical observable, the genealogical entanglement witness <X⊗W>. Phase 2 pushes that witness to the coherence ceiling of live 156-qubit Heron-r2 hardware along three axes: the unitary genealogy (idealized, no dissipation), the faithful amplitude-damping death channel swept in width, and — the last gap — that same death channel swept in generational depth. Every ceiling of the pre-existing design is now located, not bracketed.

Unitary axis: entanglement depth W* = 24 = 48 qubits (k=2σ), ~6× the paper's ~4-qubit origin; W=32 dead. A final damping-channel rebuild lifted the W=2 witness from the earlier 0.55 to +0.814 and pushed the width ceiling far out: certified at k=3σ through W=9 = 19 qubits (+0.171), dead at W=10 (+0.022, exactly on the gate). Depth axis: with real death firing every generation the genealogy survives 5 generations (W=6, steps=5, +0.138 k=3σ) and collapses at the 6th (+0.013). The [4,8) bracket from the prior draft is closed on both counts.

Honest framing throughout: this is scale · faithfulness · certification — not a speedup. These are the absolute limits of the old design; passing them needs new biology (P3), not more qubits.

1 · The three axes

Scale is qubit-cheap; the walls are decoherence and the death operator's per-generation entropy

Each individual costs two qubits; the amplitude-damping death mode adds one shared bath ancilla. Width is therefore never the bottleneck — a genealogy of W=77 would fill a 156-qubit chip. What ends the witness is entropy: decoherence along the unitary axis, and, once the faithful death channel is on, the deliberate dissipation that channel injects at every generation.

P2 measures the witness on three axes. The unitary axis (§2) finds how far the idealized reproduction coheres in width. The damping-width axis (§3) re-asks the width question with real death in the loop. The damping-depth axis (§4) holds width fixed and stacks generations, each one firing the death channel — the harshest test, and the last gap the epic had left open. A final rebuild of the damping channel pushed both damping ceilings well past the earlier draft's numbers.

2 · The unitary scale ceiling

W* = 24 = 48 qubits, ~6× the paper

On the unitary genealogy the witness decays roughly geometrically with W. Against the noiseless ideal curve — which saturates near 0.9 (its small droop is modeled mutation, Ry(θ), not decoherence) — the hardware curve separates decoherence from model-intrinsic decay. The last width clearing the k=2σ significance gate is W=24; W=32 is dead.

3 · The amplitude-damping death channel — width

Final channel rebuild: width ceiling located at W* = 9 = 19 qubits

Switching death from a unitary rotation to a faithful amplitude-damping channel (a shared bath ancilla, dissipation per generation) is the first genuine biological operator of the epic — and the harshest noise channel in the model. The original build was unstable at W=2 (runs spanning −0.04 to +0.35); a first fix reached +0.545; the earlier draft then had W=8 dead (+0.001) and bracketed the ceiling to W ∈ [4, 8). A final channel rebuild closed that gap: W=2 now reads +0.814 (up from 0.55), and the witness holds up in width far past the old bracket — W=6 +0.542, W=8 +0.468, and W=9 +0.171, all certified at k=3σ. The collapse is then sharp: W=10 = +0.022 sits exactly on the k=2σ gate (dead) and W=12 = −0.005 is firmly in the noise floor. The ceiling is located, not bracketed: W* = 9 = 19 qubits (18 for the genealogy + 1 shared bath ancilla). Separable null ≤ 3×10⁻⁷ across the whole sweep.

4 · The amplitude-damping death channel — depth

Real death survives 5 generations; the 6th collapses

The last gap was generational depth: hold width fixed and stack steps, firing the death channel every generation. This is strictly harsher than width — each added step is a fresh round of dissipation on already-decohered state. At W=6 the genealogy stays certified through 5 generations (+0.138, k=3σ) and collapses at the 6th (+0.013, dead). The W=5 pair brackets the k=3σ→k=2σ softening (steps=4 +0.111 clears k=3σ; steps=5 +0.049 holds only k=2σ), confirming the edge is the step count, not the width. Depth is the tighter of the two damping walls: the design buys 9 individuals wide at one generation, but only 6 wide by the fifth.

5 · Confound & significance

The decay is physical, not a dead-edge artifact

Every hardware run enforced the fail-closed chain-quality gate (abort if two-qubit error > 0.05 or readout > 0.15), so the witness decay is device physics, not a broken edge. The separable null ∏i<Xi> — what a classical measure-and-resend device returns — stayed ≤ 3×10⁻⁷ across all widths and depths on all three axes, orders of magnitude under the CD-3 gate of 0.05. The sharp W=9→W=10 cliff and the steps=5→steps=6 cliff both land within a single unit while calibration held steady, confirming the collapse is the death channel's entropy, not chain drift. The witness is the sole quantum claim; every diagonal metric (alive-count, deepest-lineage, phenotype ⟨σ_z⟩) has an exact classical surrogate and carries none.

6 · Decision gate (AC-P2.4)

Every ceiling of the old design is located — the design is exhausted, P3 needs new biology

Gate ruling — all gaps closed

Unitary scale axis: exhausted. W* = 24 proven, W=32 dead, decay geometric with no structure. Further unitary width is "more qubits, same biology."

Damping-width axis: exhausted. The final channel certifies through W=9 = 19 qubits and dies at W=10 (on the gate). Located, not bracketed.

Damping-depth axis: exhausted. Real death survives 5 generations (W=6, +0.138) and collapses at the 6th (+0.013). The [4,8) bracket the prior draft left open is now closed on both width and depth, so the pre-existing design has been driven to its absolute limit. Error mitigation could nudge these edges out a width or a generation, but the design's biology is fully mapped. The P3 gate opens: further advance requires new biology (germ/soma split, multi-organism competition), not more qubits on this circuit.
