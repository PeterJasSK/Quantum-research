1. CONVENTIONS USED FOR EVERY ROW
======================================================================================================================
1.1 Break-even (exactly as BRIEF-screening): S* = t_Q / t_C with t_C = t_propose_C + t_energy and t_Q = t_latency + t_energy.
    optimistic t_latency = 0.03 s (D-Wave reverse-anneal step) for any row whose H_prob is 2-body (QUBO-embeddable);
      0.1 s (Helios in-program loop, estimate) for rows that need multi-body H_prob (LABS, LDPC checks, polar, protograph,
      d_min search), where an annealer needs aux spins and the gate model is the natural fit. Caveat: the annealer step is a
      DIFFERENT proposal (Arai-Kadowaki type, arXiv:2502.08060); its S is not the pilot S. So the optimistic column is a
      device-latency bound, not a like-for-like proposal.
    realistic t_latency = 3 s (IBM Heron session per step, Marshall 2026 derived). No row is annealer-native in the brief's
      sense, so the 0.5 s D-Wave realistic figure is not used.
    pessimistic = S*_realistic / F, F = (1 - 2e-3)^N_2q.
1.2 N_2q rule [OWN, anchored on the brief default N_2q = 800 at 10-12 dense spins]:
      dense couplings: N_2q = 800 x (g/11)^2 ; sparse/local couplings: N_2q = 800 x (g/11) ; g = qubits in one proposal
      circuit (coarse-grained group size where one is used, the smallest group the ideation agent named, to give each idea its
      best shot). This is GENEROUS to quantum: the repo's 5-spin SK Trotter circuit is already 823 CZ, and B4's own swap-network
      estimate (1.5 n^2 CNOT per layer x 10-100 layers) gives 3.6e3-3.6e4 at n = 16. Multi-body rows use the dense rule as a
      lower bound; LABS gets an explicit estimate (section 2).
1.3 F caveat [OWN]: a depolarised proposal is roughly F x Q_ideal + (1 - F) x Q_near-uniform, and at HIGH/MEDIUM T the uniform
    part is not useless, so S/F over-states the erosion there. The convention is applied anyway for comparability; it never
    changes a verdict below, because every verdict is already decided by the realistic column or by the classical competitor.
1.4 "Beats best classical?" column = wall-clock win at a size an engineer cares about. "N (steps UNKNOWN)" marks science rows
    where the iteration-count question is open but wall-clock is closed by S*.
1.5 Batching (up to 4,000 circuits/s on IBM, t_Q per chain-step about 2.5e-4 s) is applied only where the application
    genuinely needs many independent chains: B4-05 (labels over many channel draws) and B4-20 (SNR sweep). It is irrelevant for
    expensive-energy rows (t_E dominates) and is stated where used.
1.6 Measured/estimated S: pilot SK n=10 vs best(local, uniform) HIGH 1.3x, MEDIUM 3-8x, LOW 25-40x [PILOT]; vs PT/cluster/
    specialised solver UNKNOWN everywhere (no published comparison). No row has a measured S against its best classical method.

N1. B4-04 (+ B4-03 slice): Nishimori-line transfer test.
  Instances (20 per point, fixed seeds):
    (i) planted MIMO BPSK: x0 uniform in {+1,-1}^n, H real Gaussian entries N(0, 1/N_r), N_r = round(n/alpha),
        alpha in {0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0}; y = H x0 + w, w ~ N(0, sigma^2 I), sigma^2 in {0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0};
        E(x) = ||y - Hx||^2, beta_m = 1/(2 sigma^2).
    (ii) planted Sourlas 3-spin code: M = 2n random triples, J_ijk = x0_i x0_j x0_k flipped with prob p in {0.05, 0.1, 0.2},
        E = -sum J_ijk s_i s_j s_k, beta_m = 0.5 ln((1-p)/p).
    (iii) SK control: qmc.sk_instance, beta in {0.3, 1.0} (reproduce pilot).
  n in {6, 8, 10, 12}. Temperatures beta/beta_m in {0.5, 1, 2, 4} (4 = ML-like off-Nishimori control).
  Proposals: quantum_Q with H_prob = diagonal E (alpha from qmc.alpha_from_energy, gamma U[0.25,0.6], t U[2,20], averaged over
    the qmc.draw_params set used in the pilot); short-time quantum control t U[0.3,1.5]; local_Q; uniform_Q; mixture_Q 0.5 local
    + 0.5 uniform. PT (4 replicas, geometric from beta_m/4 to target) via qmc.parallel_tempering on chains.
  Observables: exact spectral gap (qmc.gap) per proposal; R = gap_Q / max(gap_local, gap_uniform, gap_mix); fitted exponent
    k from gap about 2^(-k n) over n = 6..12; at n = 12, IAT (qmc.iat) of overlap q = x.x0/n and of E per energy evaluation
    for the quantum chain vs PT (count all replica evaluations), 1e5 steps, 10 chains; bimodality of q histogram for alpha
    at least 1.5 (B4-03 window).
  KILL criterion: at beta = beta_m, median R at most 1.5 for every alpha up to 1.5 in ensembles (i) and (ii) AND
    k_Q/k_best at least 0.8 -> close the telecom/inference sampling vertical (B4-02/03/05/07/11 final KILL).
  CONFIRM criterion: median R at least 3 at beta_m with k_Q at most 0.6 k_best on at least one ensemble/alpha slice AND
    IAT_PT / IAT_Q at least 2 per energy evaluation at n = 12 -> promote B4-03, reopen B4-05/B4-11 as quantum-inspired arms.
    Wall-clock stays closed (S* 1e8 realistic) regardless.
  Cost: 4096-state eigenproblems at n = 12; days of CPU.

N2. B1-10 kill test for the whole surrogate-in-U, expensive-energy family (covers B1-09/11/12/14/17, informs B4-07).
  Instances: synthetic proxy, because no EM data sets are on disk: E_FM(s) = rank-8 factorisation-machine QUBO with random
    Gaussian factors and linear terms (dense J); E_true(s) = E_FM(s) + sigma_r R(s), R = random 3-body plus 4-body terms
    normalised to unit variance under uniform s. Every evaluation of E_true counts as one expensive call. 10 instances.
  n = 12 exact (4096 states); n = 64 chains with P2 coarse-grained random groups of g = 12 (rest frozen as effective fields
    for the FM part).
  Temperatures: beta_c = specific-heat peak of E_true at n = 12 (MEDIUM) and 2 beta_c; sigma_r chosen so that
    beta sigma_r in {0.25, 0.5, 1, 2}.
  Kernels (all target pi_true exactly): (a) DA with quantum_Q(H_prob = E_FM), stage 1 on E_FM, stage 2 on
    pi(s')pi_FM(s)/(pi(s)pi_FM(s')); (b) DA with local_Q; (c) DA with uniform_Q; (d) surrogate transition: proposal
    P_FM^k (classical Metropolis on E_FM at the same beta), k in {1, 10, 100, 1000}, one expensive call per proposal;
    (e) independence draw from exact pi_FM (k to infinity); (f, B1-14 arm) 4-replica PT on pi_true with kernel (a) or (d) in
    the two coldest replicas.
  Observables: n = 12: exact spectral gap of the composite pi_true kernel divided by expected expensive calls per step (gap per
    call); outer acceptance vs beta sigma_r against a = 2 Phi(-beta sigma_r / sqrt 2). n = 64: IAT in expensive calls of
    E_true and of design overlap, 2e4 calls x 10 chains.
  KILL criterion: best classical kernel (b-e) reaches at least 0.8 x the quantum gap per call at every (beta, beta sigma_r)
    -> B1-10/B1-14 final KILL and the whole family closes.
  CONFIRM criterion: quantum gap per call at least 2x best classical at some beta sigma_r up to 1 at n = 12, AND IAT ratio at
    least 2 at n = 64 -> fund a real pilot (openEMS 8x8 pixel patch, FM refit epochs, then hardware with g = 12-16).
  Code: about 50 lines for DA and surrogate-transition matrices on top of qmc.mh_matrix / qmc.spectral_gap.

N3. B4-15 LABS proposal-kernel exponent study.
  Instances: LABS for N = 6..14 (exact gaps; one instance per N, E = sum_{k=1}^{N-1} C_k^2, ground energies from the known
    optimum table); TTS runs for N = 10..18.
  Temperatures: beta in {0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0} (E integer units); LOW defined per N as the betas where the local gap is
    below 1e-3 at N = 14.
  Proposals: quantum_Q with the true diagonal (2-body plus 4-body) E; quantum_Q with the 2-body-only part of E as surrogate
    (terms with j = i + k, still exact accept); local_Q; uniform_Q; mixture 0.5/0.5.
  Observables: exact gaps -> exponent k per proposal and beta; TTS: 8-replica PT, quantum proposal (statevector sampled,
    cached per state) in the 2 coldest replicas vs all-local PT, steps to first ground-state hit, 50 seeds per N, fitted base
    b of steps-to-solution.
  KILL criterion: k_Q at least 0.8 k_local at LOW beta, or PT+Q steps base b at least 1.30 (no margin over MTS literature 1.34)
    -> B4-15 final KILL.
  CONFIRM criterion: k_Q at most 0.5 k_local and b at most 1.25 at N up to 18 -> write up as a quantum-inspired LABS kernel
    result, explicitly comparing to arXiv:2308.02342 and arXiv:2511.04553; wall-clock still lost (S* 1e8 realistic).
  Caveat: MTS steps and PT steps are not the same unit; the comparison to 1.34 is context, the falsifiable part is vs local PT.

Not nominated: B4-07 (only after N1 or N2 is positive), B1-15 (hardware), B1-18/K4 (quantum-inspired, low payoff).
