SCREENING BRIEF (Wave 3). Read BRIEF-common.txt first.

SCREENING CRITERIA per idea: (1) is mixing really the bottleneck in the regime the application needs; (2) which regime and why;
(3) Ising/QUBO/Potts mapping + embedding cost; (4) energy cost per step; (5) do fewer steps beat slower steps (break-even);
(6) hardware fit now / public roadmap <=3 yr, with or without coarse-graining; (7) beats BEST classical for that regime (PT, cluster,
specialised solvers, delayed acceptance with the same surrogate) not just Metropolis; (8) real engineer payoff; (9) first test in days?

BREAK-EVEN CONVENTION (use exactly, so rows are comparable):
 t_C = t_propose_C + t_energy, with the best classical method's per-step cost (cheap dense Ising ~30 ns per proposal at n<=100,
   ~0.1-0.5 us at n=1000, A5 bench; sparse lattice ~0.15 ns/flip CPU, ~2-3 ps GPU/FPGA).
 t_Q = t_latency_and_overhead + t_energy:
   optimistic  = best published per-step latency for the best-fitting device: D-Wave 0.03 s; Helios in-program loop 0.1 s
                 (estimate); IBM 2 s.
   realistic   = IBM Heron session ~3 s per step (queue excluded; Marshall 2026 derived) or D-Wave 0.5 s if annealer-native.
   pessimistic = realistic t_Q AND the step advantage is eroded by noise: required ideal S = S*_realistic / F,
                 F = (1 - 2e-3)^(N_2q) circuit fidelity proxy, N_2q = two-qubit gate count of one proposal circuit
                 (if unknown use N_2q ~ 800 for a 10-12 spin dense Trotter circuit on heavy-hex (repo: 5-spin SK = 823 CZ, so dense
                 grows fast) -> F ~ 0.2; state your N_2q).
 S* = t_Q / t_C. Report S* as "optimistic/realistic/pessimistic" in scientific notation (e.g. 1e6/1e8/5e8).
 Batching: if the application needs many independent chains, t_Q per chain-step may be divided by batch size up to ~4,000
   circuits/s on IBM (state it explicitly when used).

MEASURED/ESTIMATED S per regime (until Wave 4 reports, use and label): own pilot SK n=10 quantum vs best(local,uniform):
 HIGH beta~0.1: 1.3x; MEDIUM beta~0.3-0.5: 3-8x; LOW beta>=1: 25-40x. Versus PT / cluster: UNKNOWN (no published comparison).
 Literature: P1 k_q 0.264 vs 0.94; P2 T=10 k 0.087 vs 0.26 local; Christmann: classical TN emulation keeps advantage at n<=9.

Definitions: wall-clock likelihood = P(within 3 years, on existing or publicly roadmapped hardware, beats best classical in real time
at a size an engineer cares about). Steps likelihood = P(real iteration-count advantage vs best classical at that size, ignoring time).

mostly 0-3%); a low result is fine. Hardware fit 0 = no device can run it, 1 = toy size only, 2 = meaningful size with coarse-graining,
3 = meaningful size natively.
