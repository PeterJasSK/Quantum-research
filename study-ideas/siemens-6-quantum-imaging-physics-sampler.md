# Siemens Idea 6 — Sample the Physics, Not the Picture: can a quantum circuit sample the *imaging forward-physics* (Compton scatter / photon transport) to make physically-faithful synthetic data — and is that quantum sampler certifiably non-classical or classically fakeable?

**Tag: QUANTUM · siemens · effort: medium-high**

## Pitch
Every quantum medical-generative paper found in the 2024–2026 literature mimics image *pixels* (QIGL, hybrid quantum-classical latent diffusion arXiv 2508.09903, quantum-enhanced diffusion augmentation). That lane is crowded and loses to classical diffusion. The non-obvious move: don't imitate the picture — use a quantum circuit (conditional Born machine / discrete-time quantum walk) to **sample the stochastic physics that produces the picture** — Compton scatter angle/energy, photon transport, detector response. Synthetic training data then comes from the *forward physics*, not from copying pixels. CERN openlab already proved the core mechanic on real IBM hardware: a conditional QCBM sampling muon-detector *scatter* (arXiv 2205.07674, 2022). No one has done this for medical imaging — it is open ground — and it maps onto Siemens' crown jewel: photon-counting CT (NAEOTOM) *is* photon-interaction physics. The honest spine is the repo's own weapon: a sampler's output distribution is classical, so instead of asserting a non-classicality certificate we *test* it — apply the entanglement-witness / classical-surrogate instrument (thesis-4 / CriticalQuantumLife tooling) to decide whether the quantum scatter-sampler does genuinely non-classical work or is classically fakeable.
**Paper strength score: 74/100** — non-obvious, hardware-precedented, perfectly Siemens-shaped, and honest-by-design because the certification verdict is itself the result; docked because mature classical MC physics engines (Geant4/GATE/MC-GPU/DeepDRR) are a very high bar and the quantum "win" is conditional-cheapness, not a speed or fidelity beat.

## How it becomes a study
**Research question:** Can a few-qubit quantum circuit sample a conditional imaging-physics distribution (Compton scatter angle/energy given photon energy) faithfully on real hardware, and does that sampler carry a certifiable non-classical resource during generation or does an explicit classical surrogate reproduce it?
**Hypothesis (falsifiable):** A conditional QCBM/DTQW reproduces the 1-D Klein–Nishina scatter distribution to within sampling error on hardware, **and** a classical surrogate (matched measure-and-resample / shallow classical sampler) reproduces the same output distribution — i.e. the generation is classically fakeable and the non-classicality witness is null, exactly as the "advantage is the exception" ethos predicts.
**Method:** (1) Build the reference: analytic Klein–Nishina + a Geant4/GATE 1-D Compton scatter distribution. (2) Train a conditional Born machine (reuse QuantumTree's generative + certification code) and/or a DTQW scatter sampler to reproduce it, conditioned on incident photon energy; run statevector → noisy sim → IBM Heron hardware (CERN-style). (3) Certification arm: compute the entanglement witness on the generator state and build the explicit classical surrogate; measure whether the witness is positive and whether the surrogate matches.
**Baseline:** Classical Monte-Carlo physics engine (Geant4/GATE) for the physics reference; a matched classical sampler (KDE / shallow model / measure-and-resample surrogate) for the certification control.
**Metrics:** physics-fidelity of samples to the Geant4/analytic reference (KS / Wasserstein); conditioning quality (does the distribution shift correctly with photon energy); **witness value** on the generator state (certificate present/absent); surrogate-match gap (classically fakeable yes/no); qubits/depth to reach fidelity.
**Novel contribution:** First quantum sampler of *medical imaging forward-physics* (scatter/transport) rather than image pixels, with an explicit, honest verdict on whether the quantum resource is non-classical — reframing synthetic-data generation as physics-sampling + a certification question.

## Bull case / Bear case / Likely outcome / Value if null
**Bull:** You own a genuinely new framing (quantum physics-sampling for medical data) with a real hardware precedent and a certification verdict no pixel-GAN paper can offer — and it points straight at Siemens' photon-counting flagship.
**Bear:** Classical MC physics engines are decades-mature; "faithful but classically fakeable" could read as "why bother," unless the certification verdict and the conditional-surrogate cheapness are framed as the contribution.
**Likely:** QCBM reproduces the 1-D scatter distribution on hardware; the witness comes back null and a classical surrogate matches it — an honest "this generation is classically simulable" result, exactly the repo's signature finding.
**Value if null:** A null witness *is* the publishable result — it demarcates, for a real Siemens imaging task, where quantum sampling does and does not buy non-classical structure, feeding directly the "advantage is the exception" thesis and Siemens' 10-year scaling judgement.
