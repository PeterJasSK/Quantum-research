# Thesis Idea 1 — The Swap-Routed Quantum Virus: how far can inherited quantum coherence travel across a "stone wall" before a classical infector fakes it, and is that surviving structure certified entanglement?

**Tag: QUANTUM · thesis (artificial-life / QDEP family) · effort: high · QC: yes**

## Pitch
An instruction-driven "virus" (a 3-locus genome: one quantum witness locus + two classical loci) infects a chain of genealogy blocks A→B→C separated by wide "stone walls" (inter-block gaps). We measure the **cross-block connected-GHZ transmission witness** `⟨X^⊗2W⟩ − ⟨X^⊗W⟩_A · ⟨X^⊗W⟩_B` as a **decay curve versus inter-block distance**, and — the new thesis-grade chapter — replace the ambiguous witness with a genuine **entanglement certificate** so the headline is "measurable entanglement transmission," not merely "measurable quantum structure." The mechanism is already proven on real hardware (P0 kill-gate: signal alive at 0.215 @ d=2, still 0.060 @ d=48). This is the biggest, most impressive, and safest realization of the "quantum virus" vision — with long-range teleportation demoted to a rigorously-documented **negative control** (it has been refuted three separate times as a routing strategy).
**Paper strength score: 80/100** — de-risked mechanism + a novel, memorable model the author already near-solely owns; docked because "artificial life" reads as niche to a hard-QI committee unless the entanglement-certification chapter carries the rigor, and it carries IBM-hardware queue/calendar dependency.

## How it becomes a study
**Research question:** As an instruction-driven virus infects blocks A→B→C across stone walls, how does the connected-GHZ transmission witness decay with inter-block distance, at what distance does a classical measure-and-resend infector become statistically indistinguishable, and can the surviving cross-block correlation be certified as genuine entanglement rather than generic quantum structure?
**Hypothesis:** The swap-routed transmission witness stays above the classical measure-and-resend null out to a finite critical distance d\*, and below d\* a Bell/entanglement-witness inequality is violated (certifying entanglement); above d\* both collapse to the classical floor. (Falsifiable: if the certified-entanglement inequality never clears noise even at d=2, the entanglement claim dies and the honest result is a bounded upper limit on certifiable transmission depth.)
**Method:** Build P1 (`stage5_virus.py`): 3-locus genome, static Darwinian selection, three W12 stone-wall blocks, **swap-routed** A→B→C infection (the P0-proven route). Sweep inter-block distance; run matched arms on Heron-r2 (`ibm_kingston`/`ibm_fez`): (i) quantum-coherent infector, (ii) classical measure-and-resend infector (the null), (iii) teleport-routed infector (documented negative control). Then harden the witness into an entanglement certificate (staggered-parity / witness-inequality construction flagged as required in `CONCLUSION_ENTANGLEMENT_CORRELATION.md §5`).
**Baseline:** The classical measure-and-resend infector on the same QRNG entropy stream — provably reproduces any diagonal/coherence-only signal, so any surviving gap is non-classical by construction.
**Metrics:** transmission-witness value vs distance (decay curve); critical distance d\* where quantum arm meets the classical null; entanglement-witness inequality margin in σ vs distance; deepest block width W and generation count that still clears 2σ.
**Novel contribution:** The first measured **decay curve of certified quantum-genealogical transmission across physical hardware gaps** — a virus whose inherited coherence you can watch die across a wall, with an explicit "it is entanglement, not just quantum structure" certificate and teleport-routing reported as a clean bounded negative.

## Bull case / Bear case / Likely outcome / Value if null
**Bull:** P0 already proved the mechanism on metal, so the risky discovery is behind you; you deliver a clean decay curve + an entanglement certificate + the most visually striking figure in the repo, on a model literally nobody else works on. The "6× deeper than the 2018 paper" lineage gives you an instant, defensible novelty framing.
**Bear:** The entanglement-certification inequality may not clear hardware noise even at short range, forcing you back to the weaker "quantum structure" claim; the "artificial life" wrapper can read as a toy to a committee that wants hard QI, so the framing must foreground the witness/certificate rigor.
**Likely outcome:** A real decay curve with a finite d\*; entanglement certified at short range and lost at long range; teleport confirmed dead as routing. A clean, quotable, moderately-strong result with a standout figure.
**Value if null:** Even if entanglement never certifies, you publish a bounded upper limit on certifiable quantum-transmission depth on this hardware generation, plus the third independent refutation of teleport-routing — both are honest, citable numbers, and the model/witness framework is itself a contribution.
